science Extraction Protocol

The 1:2 Dial Standard

We dial in our grinders four times per shift to compensate for bar humidity and ambient temperature. All espresso recipes are extracted at 9 bars between 199.5°F and 201.2°F, targeting an 18.5g in, 38g out parameter across 28–31 seconds.

Mateo Vance Head of Roasting & Quality Control
